# Battery

A battery gauge drawn like Android's: a rounded cell filling from the left, a
bolt while charging, and the figure beside it.

```html
<syn-battery [level]="62" [charging]="false" />
```

<Canvas of={stories.Default} />

<Controls of={stories.Default} />

## One thing to read, not three

The drawing is `aria-hidden` and the host carries the whole meaning as its
accessible name — _"Battery 62%, discharging"_. Announcing the cell, the bolt
and the number separately would give a screen reader three fragments and no
sentence.

Inline SVG rather than an asset, for the same reason the Razer mark is: an
`<img>` is an isolated document and no rule of ours could colour it.

## States

<Canvas of={stories.Charging} />
<Canvas of={stories.Low} />

Below 20% the gauge turns to `--syn-error`, figure included. **Charging is
never low** — it is its own state, and a good one, so it stays on
`--syn-success` even at 4%:

<Canvas of={stories.ChargingFromEmpty} />

Otherwise the charge takes `--syn-primary`, like every other gauge in the
library, and the shell and figure stay on `--syn-on-surface-variant`: they are
furniture, not the reading.

<Canvas of={stories.Range} />

## Details worth keeping

An empty battery keeps a sliver of fill, so it still reads as a battery rather
than a bare outline. The figure uses tabular figures **and** a minimum width —
tabular alone still lets the gauge shuffle between two and three digits. And a
level outside 0–100 is brought back into range rather than drawn past the cell.
