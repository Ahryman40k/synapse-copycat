## Where this is

Synapse is **in development**. It drives Razer peripherals through the
OpenRazer daemon, and the rendering engine, the groups and the interface around
them work end to end.

What it does not do yet is stated plainly on every page rather than left for
you to discover. Start with [what actually works](/features/).
