The hard part

Setting a wallpaper on Linux means driving whatever your desktop uses, and there is no common way in. This will be a set of adapters, not a call:

GNOME
GSettings — and the dark variant separately, or switching theme brings the old one back.
KDE Plasma
No setting to write: it takes a Plasma script, evaluated by the shell over DBus.
XFCE
xfconf-query, once per monitor and per workspace — the property path contains both.
swww, hyprpaper
Their own client or their own socket, and each one different.

Whichever of those land will say which ones were found on your machine, rather than offering one control that silently does nothing on three desktops out of four.
